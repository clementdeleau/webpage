AI & high-performance computing

I use neural networks to read sensors better and to design photonic devices faster, training them on supercomputers and GPU clusters.

Machine learning for fiber sensing

A long-period fiber grating responds to bending along its whole length, but untangling that spectral response by hand is hard. Pairing the grating with machine-learning numerical analysis made it possible to perform distributed curvature sensing from the measured spectra.

Calibration networks on supercomputers

I train calibration networks that map raw optical measurements to physical quantities. Large training runs and hyperparameter sweeps run on supercomputer and GPU-cluster resources rather than a desktop.
